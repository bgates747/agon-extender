PORT-008 second forward-run diagnostic summary

Run PORT-008-2026-09-01-02-10-50Z used the experimental unversioned
corrections for the first run's ADL storage overlap and P4 direction-enable
ownership. The P4 completed its controlled startup, selected FWD_OE_N low and
REV_OE_N high, returned HTTP 200, and the Author confirmed the r01 harness and
LA-03 probes before the Agon cold boot.

The Agon again reported an error executing autoexec.txt line 1 followed by
Internal error. Line 1 was EMOS MODE EXTENDED. The route was not committed and
P8VDU.BIN did not execute.

The 8 MHz capture contains the first physical General Poll attempt. It sampled
three falling CLOCK edges during the initial 0.375 us VALID_N assertion.
READY_N remained low at the capture end, so the P4 receive did not complete and
EMOS reached its completion timeout. FWD_OE_N remained low as selected.
REV_OE_N was low for one 125 ns sample; that isolated sample is retained as an
analyzer failure but is not evidence of the long direction contention seen in
the first run.

Source comparison found a second independent EMOS defect. The hardware-proven
legacy PRX-06 zero-delay sender asserts VALID_N while CLOCK is high before the
byte loop. It loads and writes each data byte during that high phase, drives
the falling sample edge, and raises CLOCK after every byte. The PORT-008 EMOS
sender instead changed the data, wrote CLOCK high, and immediately wrote it
low. The resulting high phases were shorter than the analyzer's 125 ns sample
period and did not provide the qualified PARLIO clock cadence. The observed
three rather than four sampled edges and unreleased READY_N are consistent
with that implementation deviation.

The original validator checked only that a Port C data write preceded two Port
D writes. It did not require a pre-loop VALID_N/CLOCK-high write, distinguish
the falling and rising clock phases, or require that data loading separate the
high and falling writes. The malformed linked image therefore passed.

The pending correction now mirrors the proven PRX-06 sequence: assert VALID_N
with CLOCK high once before the loop; write one byte while CLOCK remains high;
drive one falling and one rising Port D write per byte; and leave the record at
VALID_N inactive with CLOCK high. Source and synthetic regressions require that
shape, and the linked-image verifier confirms it in the emitted instructions.
The complete EMOS build and all 55 repository tests pass. The resulting
114,085-byte unversioned image has SHA-256
8f659845c24a9b328f6791a7ac75a2b820df254bc601517d1b2741ed7999987b.
It has not yet been installed or physically tested.

This failed run establishes that the first ADL storage correction allowed the
four-byte sender to reach its physical byte loop and that P4 direction control
removed the first run's long overlap. It does not establish a complete General
Poll record, READY release, route commitment, visible fixture execution, or a
browser-frame result.
