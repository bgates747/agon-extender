After a manual Agon reset restored the original stock-VDP/failed-EMOS startup
state, the same identity-gated P4 observer halted the eZ80 at PC 0009EA in
_wait_timer0. The General Poll completion byte remained zero, UART0 had no
received byte, and the target state reproduced the first capture.

The coherent one-shot read UART0's baud-rate divisor before any P4 reboot or
change of halt epoch. LCR was 03 and the divisor was 000B; the diagnostic
restored LCR and AF/MB before continuing. At the Agon's 18.432 MHz system clock,
the official 1,152,000-baud VDP link requires divisor 0001. Divisor 11 produces
approximately 104,727 baud and cannot communicate with the stock VDP.

The observed value exactly matches an AgonDev portability defect in the
inherited UART expression. Its 16 * 1,152,000 product was evaluated at native
24-bit width before assignment to UINT32, wrapping 0x01194000 to 0x00194000;
18,432,000 / 1,654,784 truncates to 11. This identifies the immediate physical
boot blocker without attributing it to intended stock MOS behavior.
