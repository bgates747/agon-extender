PORT-008 forward-only run failed before the visible fixture executed.

The Author cold-booted the Agon after the P4 browser endpoint and analyzer were
ready. MOS reported an error executing /autoexec.txt line 1, followed by
"Internal error." Line 1 was EMOS MODE EXTENDED, so EMOS never committed the
Exclusive Extended route and line 2, P8VDU.BIN, did not run. No browser-frame
claim is available from this run.

The 8 MHz analyzer capture contains 4,000,000 samples. It sampled one bounded
135.900 ms active-low VALID_N window, READY_N low throughout the complete
500 ms capture, no CLOCK edge, and a forbidden 135.8015 ms interval with both
FWD_OE_N and REV_OE_N low. The absence of a sampled CLOCK edge does not prove
that no pulse shorter than the 125 ns sample interval occurred. The direction-
enable overlap is independently disqualifying under light2-harness-r01, but its
physical cause is not assigned by this run.

The EMOS failure mechanism is proven in the exact deployed linked image. In
ADL mode, LD (_port008_length),BC and LD BC,(_port008_length) transfer all
24 bits of BC. Source reserved only two bytes for _port008_length at 0x0BC2FA;
the next symbol, _port008_idle_low, begins at 0x0BC2FC. The store at linked
address 0x00173C therefore overwrites _port008_idle_low with BCU. For this
four-byte General Poll, BCU is zero, replacing the saved inactive-VALID control
byte with zero. EMOS consequently reasserts VALID_N in its release-wait loop,
the P4 does not complete and release READY_N, and EMOS reaches its completion
timeout. PORT008_send returns 2; the command path interprets 2 as FatFS
FR_INT_ERR, whose MOS message is "Internal error." This exactly accounts for
the screen report and the long active transaction sampled by the analyzer.

Existing checks missed the defect for two distinct reasons. Fab does not model
this external Port C/Port D-to-P4 transport, and the graphical/runtime gate
exercised EMOS's fake adapter rather than EMOS MODE EXTENDED through the
fixed-purpose physical adapter. The physical-profile build and linked checker
did run, but they verified GPIO constants, control-flow order, General Poll
bytes, and linked instruction order. They did not compare BSS symbol spans
with the 24-bit operands used by linked LD instructions. The synthetic
disassembly unit fixture omitted the length store and reload entirely.

The P4 candidate has a separate unresolved defect or wiring-observation gap.
The r01 authority assigns GPIO15 to FWD_OE_N and GPIO21 to REV_OE_N and forbids
both being low. ForwardParallelStream configures the data, CLOCK, VALID_N, and
READY_N pins but does not configure GPIO15 or GPIO21. The capture sampled both
active during the failed transaction. Source omission and sampled overlap are
facts; whether the observed levels were caused by that omission, wiring,
probe attachment, or another electrical interaction remains unresolved.

The run also deviated from the intended observer sequence. HTTP returned 200
before capture, then opening the P4 USB Serial/JTAG endpoint produced a fresh
P4 ROM/application boot. The P4 again reached receiver startup and HTTP-ready
before the captured transaction, but a rerun must deliberately open and hold
the observer through a controlled P4 startup, then wait for receiver and HTTP
readiness before authorizing the Agon boot. A late observer open is forbidden.

No firmware correction or retry belongs to this run. Before another physical
attempt, EMOS needs width-correct length storage plus a linked symbol-span
regression; P4 firmware needs an actor-explicit, fail-safe r01 direction-enable
lifecycle plus deterministic checks; and both corrected images need new build
IDs from clean committed inputs. The exact harness/probe state must then be
reconfirmed and a new run ID assigned.
