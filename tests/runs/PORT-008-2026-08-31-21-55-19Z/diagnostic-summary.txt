PORT-008 external-ZDI failed-state capture

Run: PORT-008-2026-08-31-21-55-19Z
Outcome: PASS for the bounded capture objective; not qualification evidence.

The P4 observed the original hardware failure while the Agon's onboard ESP32
continued running stock VDP v2.14.1 Dressing Gown. Three ZDI identity reads returned product
0007, revision AA, and the eZ80 was initially running.

The one-shot capture halted the eZ80 at PC 0009EA. The exact candidate map and
machine code place this at the first Timer 0 counter read in _wait_timer0; the
stack returns through _wait_ESP32 and then MOS startup. The captured timer had
reached zero, so this is not evidence of a Timer 0 deadlock. It is one sample
from EMOS repeatedly waiting because the General Poll completion byte gp
remained zero.

UART0 was enabled with hardware flow control and 8N1 framing. CTS was active,
the transmitter was empty, and no receive byte was pending. The snapshot does
not prove whether the General Poll request appeared correctly on the wire,
whether stock VDP received it, or whether a response failed electrically or in
eZ80 UART configuration. A coherent follow-up capture must read the UART
divisor in the same halt epoch and physical signal evidence remains necessary.
