How to read this evidence
  1. Points use matching pass numbers on both endpoints. Missing and failed measurements stay absent; gaps in load curves are not joined.
  2. “Median” is the median of paired per-pass medians. “95th percentile” is the median of paired per-pass p95 values. Samples are not pooled. Range whiskers span pass medians, not confidence intervals.
  3. Double-buffer completion includes the ordinary vblank-synchronised swap. A step near 16.67 or 33.33 ms can reflect waiting for a refresh opportunity.
  4. Hardware-sprite composition recurs outside the command fence. P4 presentation includes that composition; completion alone does not measure its cost.
  5. P4 HDMI submissions, application updates and the DMA scanout proxy have different scopes. A 60 Hz DMA count can scan an older framebuffer repeatedly.
  6. Clock values are nominal PRT1 /256 counts: 13.889 μs/count. The 16.67 ms budget is 1,200 counts. Measurements include frame-marker and timing instrumentation.
