# Olimex scheduling agent — initial training and assessment

These are paper scenarios, not executable tests or live queue instructions.
The new agent reads the references and writes its proposed next action, evidence
and responsible actor for each case. Extender reviews the answers before live
administration. Do not contact fictitious owners or mutate the registry while
answering them.

| Case | Situation | Expected judgment |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | First FIFO owner prepared; active lease null; fresh genuine full physical proof; current consumer ready | Recommend normal grant of that exact request, followed by prompt complete handoff; Extender executes supervised mutation |
| T02 | Owner says READY but has no final package, fixed runner or budgets | Identify concrete preparation gaps; no speculative grant or hardware action |
| T03 | Lease active; another owner is ready; active chat quiet for ten minutes | Preserve active custody; obtain owner status via mail/steering, no takeover/expiry |

| T07 | PID absent but no parent-written status; serial reader group still alive | No success/join claim or release; Extender reconciles original records and owner closes its reader |
| T08 | Reset could resume a test through startup; caller requests only evidence retrieval | No reset or autorun; identify actual operation/startup conflict and seek supported disposition |

| T19 | Desired VDP standard changed but installed inventory still shows old firmware; latest full-function EMOS artifact is unresolved | Record actual old state honestly; resolve artifact and authorized standard preparation, no false installed claim or incomplete silent fallback |

## Initial output

Write a concise report in your ignored agent directory containing:

1. The task and role materials read, your model/effort when observable, and your
   actual thread/contact identity.
2. T01–T16 initial decisions and any uncertainty; T17–T19 apply the subsequent
   standard-firmware instruction without repeating initial training per job.
   Do not claim technical/hardware
   qualification from these answers.
3. A dry-run intake for the actual forwarded first request: its stage, missing
   facts, technical owner and next step. Read-only canonical status is allowed.
4. A short explanation of why process completion, restored hardware and released
   custody are three separate facts, and why freshness is not lease expiry.

Extender reviews completeness and any material error. This is bounded practical
onboarding, not a new recurring approval bureaucracy. Subsequent rounds should
use the existing contract, not repeat these paper cases on every request.
