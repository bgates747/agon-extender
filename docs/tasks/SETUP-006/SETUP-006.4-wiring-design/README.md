# SETUP-006.4 wiring-design review

> **Historical task evidence:** This directory predates the common-UART design
> review. Its predecessor-circuit drawings and Exclusive Extended beta
> allocations do not override `light2-harness-r02`, the current frozen
> electrical authority. Use it only for provenance and the preserved r01
> forward-test assembly unless a current task cites a specific retained fact.

## Purpose

This directory contains the task-local electrical-requirements review, the
Author's completed Fritzing wiring draft, and its first deterministic review
derivative for the Agon Light 2 Extender beta assembly.

The review is firmware-led. It identifies the signals, ownership rules,
electrical conditioning, fixed board functions, conflicts, and qualification
needs before a new wiring design is accepted. It does not infer correctness
from the predecessor harness merely because that harness exists on the bench.

## Scope

1. The immediate physical-design candidate is the **Exclusive Extended** beta
   transport: eight-bit eZ80-to-P4 parallel command delivery and P4-to-eZ80
   UART response delivery.
2. **Exclusive Compatible** requires a separate stock-UART physical design.
   This work records its abstract firmware and wire-contract demands but does
   not assign its pins or adapt `light2-harness-r01` to it. That design review
   begins only after the applicable EMOS and EDP firmware requirements mature.
3. Legacy mode requires Extender to be electrically and logically absent.
   Dual mode may constrain safe defaults and resource reservations, but its
   full wiring is not being invented here.
4. Fixed and intended P4 DevKit facilities—including Ethernet, microSD, USB,
   MIPI, and possible MOD-WIFI-ESP8266 attachment—are checked for conflicts.
   Aspirational facilities do not automatically reserve every usable GPIO.
5. This work does not authorize physical rewiring, power or reset operations,
   firmware flashing, a hardware revision, or a qualification run.

## Authorities

1. Current project mode, transport, and safety decisions in SETUP-005,
   PORT-008, QUAL-002, REMED-001, and the operating-mode audit.
2. Official Agon documentation and the pinned official MOS/VDP source for
   UART, flow-control, startup, packet, and GPIO contracts.
3. Olimex ESP32-P4-DevKit Rev D1 schematic and board sources for physical
   header connectivity and onboard peripheral use.
4. `hardware/designs/light2-harness-r01/` as versioned predecessor evidence,
   not as authority for stock UART or an accepted new beta design.

## Files

1. `light2-extender-breadboard-wiring-draft.fzz` is the Author's completed
   source diagram copied from the accepted SETUP-006.3 scaffold. Its current
   SHA-256 is
   `df8b899c41b2b186af5c298a30e70178c5fad94f0430a14f7f100407172c3b54`.
   CA-2026-08-25-001 replaced only its embedded defective UART1 label metadata
   and artwork; the pre-correction hash remains in the CA and AUDIT-002
   history. Preserve the corrected source unchanged as the input to the first
   review derivative.
2. `light2-extender-breadboard-wiring-draft_v1.fzz` is the generated review
   copy. It embeds corrected P4 part `r05`, advances the composite breadboard
   part to `r05` with its A--J row vocabulary reversed to match the physical
   upside-down orientation, adds visible row letters, and seats documented
   bench part U1 `SN74HC125N` across upper-board columns 30--36. U1 is rotated
   180 degrees in this landscape view, putting pin 1 at its upper-right corner
   at F36. AUDIT-002 also makes this derivative a complete representation of
   the documented controlled-power predecessor circuit: separate Agon pin 33
   ground and pin 34 3.3-V contacts, common signal ground, logic-reference
   rails, U1 supply/bias connections, and the corrected bidirectional `PC1`
   paths are all explicit. This does not turn that predecessor topology into
   the pending fresh-design candidate.
3. `make-draft-v1.py` deterministically performs and validates that derivation.
   It refuses to operate if the completed source hash changes. The bundled U1
   graphic and separate power-contact graphics are wiring-diagram
   accommodations to the accepted scaffold, not package, connector, or PCB-
   footprint authorities. The generator includes the evidence-backed
   AUDIT-002 corrections and preserves unrelated original geometry.
4. `light2-extender-breadboard-wiring-draft_v2.fzz` is the Author's manually
   edited cosmetic derivative of audited `draft_v1`. AUDIT-002 verifies that
   all non-wire terminal net partitions are unchanged and that v2 passes the
   complete expected-circuit contract. It is not generated by
   `make-draft-v1.py`.
5. `electrical-requirements.md` is the review record and candidate allocation.
   Facts, proposals, unresolved questions, and qualification requirements must
   remain visibly distinct.
6. `gpio-ownership-and-passthrough-discussion.md` preserves the Q001 follow-on
   discussion about total GPIO demand, consumer expansion, direct-eZ80-GPIO
   compatibility, and possible mode-switched replication of transport-owned
   expansion pins. It records the accepted beta simplification boundary while
   leaving v1 multiplexing and the final pin allocation unresolved.
7. `../SETUP-006.3-fritzing/correct-uart1-labels.py` is the bounded
   CA-2026-08-25-001 migration and validation tool for the embedded Agon header
   and label-bank parts in all three current wiring drafts.

Regenerate or verify the derivative from the repository root with:

```sh
.venv/bin/python docs/tasks/SETUP-006/SETUP-006.4-wiring-design/make-draft-v1.py
.venv/bin/python docs/tasks/SETUP-006/SETUP-006.4-wiring-design/make-draft-v1.py --check
```

## Stop boundary

The work stops after presenting a documented candidate and numbered unresolved
questions. It does not advance an artifact revision or change the established
hardware profile. Author acceptance and the applicable task gates are required
before translating the candidate into authoritative connectivity or bench
work.
