# NET-009 — Pi5-driven ESP8266 comparison with Extender TCP receiver

Move the existing ESP8266's UART and power entirely to the Pi5, preserving the
Pi's existing Olimex reset circuit. Compare known one-way streams with the
NET-008 result: correct P4 UART bytes, then a500-byte gap at the TCP receiver.
The first comparison keeps Pi5 as TCP receiver to change fewer variables; the
second sends through the modem to P4 Extender's Ethernet TCP receiver. This task
records the selected experiment and wiring preparation, not a completed move,
host reconfiguration, modem flash or executed test.

alongside; record actual lengths and probe attachment points in ignored records.

## Power decision N09-D01

**Pending verification:** direct Pi3V3 supply is the simplest proposed circuit.
Pi physical1 and17 are actual3.3V supply outputs, distinct from GPIO signal pins.
The GPIO output-drive50mA aggregate limit is not a specification for the3V3 power
header's spare capacity. Conversely, the existence of a3V3 pin does not prove
headroom on this loaded Pi.

1. Before authorizing power-on, Pi5 manager/Extender identify the Pi PSU and
   current3V3 peripheral loads and establish adequate spare rail capacity.
   Espressif specifies a3.3V supply capable of at least500mA for the ESP8266;
   this is supply capability for bursts, not a claim of500mA continuous draw.
2. Confirm the existing Olimex module's exact PCB revision, schematic onboard
   decoupling and header polarity. Do not power the module from a GPIO output.
3. If direct rail capacity cannot be established, select a Pi5V-fed3.3V regulator
   rated for the load with its manufacturer's required capacitors; its input,
   output, pinout, thermal margin and exact wires must be drawn before use.
   It remains entirely Pi-powered. Do not invent a regulator pinout or parallel
   its output with the Pi3V3 rail. This fallback is not the depicted direct wire.
4. Record the chosen supply and stable voltage during actual Wi-Fi bursts before
   interpreting transfer errors. Relocating the power source is an experimental
   variable; a changed result alone cannot isolate transmitter versus supply.
