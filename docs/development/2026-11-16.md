## NET-009 wiring sheet

Added the physical ten-contact MOD-WIFI-ESP8266 UEXT socket face to the existing
Pi5 wiring sheet. The keyed orientation follows Olimex's connector drawing and
the RX/TX labels follow its modem-specific pin assignments. The Pi pin
assignments, power proposal and existing reset wiring are unchanged.

## NET-009 female connector correction

Smith’s Console8 power check exposed a left–right reversal in the wiring
sheet. Corrected the female mating-face drawing and pin-1 marker, and replaced
the ambiguous connector reference with the explicitly labeled MOD-IO2 manual.
The Pi and modem signal assignments are unchanged.

The wires now terminate on the numbered socket contacts: GND and modem TX
on the upper row, and 3.3 V and modem RX on the lower row.

## NET-009 first modem response

Smith moved the modem to Pi5. A live RP1 UART0 TX/RX-only overlay exposes
the header tty without occupying GPIO17 or changing the debug console.
AT, ATE0, CIPSTATUS and CIFSR respond at115200. The modem reports no Wi-Fi
connection or IP; the Pi login keyring is locked, so no credential was sent.
No byte-transfer arm ran. The UART is closed and P4/Olimex were untouched.

## NET-009 Pi-to-Pi modem comparison

The enrolled machine credential enabled runtime Wi-Fi association without
a manual keyring unlock. At 1,152,000 baud the Pi-driven upload fails CRC after
25 intact frames; the TCP capture matches a 300-byte deletion. The opposite
short run passes all 361 frames. Upload at 576,000 passes 10, 30 and 60 seconds
(3,405,824 payload bytes in the final run). Pi closed the UART after verified
AT cleanup, with CUR baud 576,000. No P4 or Olimex access; the planned P4 TCP
receiver comparison remains pending.
