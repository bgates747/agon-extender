# Development log — 2026-08-24

The Author accepted REMED-001 `W2D-Q09.7`. EMOS recovery/reporting code and
EDP/P4 diagnostic firmware must not send the only human or machine-readable
failure report through a failed processor, display, transport, storage device,
or physical connection when another accepted sink survives. Reporting must not
delay or block safe recovery merely to reach a preferred sink. DIAG-001 owns
the per-failure sink priorities and qualification.

The Author made bounded P4 onboard-flash crash storage a hard v1 requirement.
The optional P4 microSD may hold richer history or exports but is not a
prerequisite, and browser/local video is not durable by itself. The existing
dedicated `coredump` partition is the implementation candidate pending
DIAG-001 validation of capacity, format, native ESP-IDF coexistence, integrity,
interrupted writes, wear limits, retrieval, and truthful evidence boundaries.

The Author accepted and generalized REMED-001 `W2D-Q09.8`. EMOS is the only
supported authority for ordinary VDU routing, Extender transport hardware, and
committed mode. Applications, linked clients, TSRs, MOS Modules, and resident
services may act only through an explicit documented EMOS operation. This is a
supported-path ownership guarantee, not adversarial isolation from deliberate
unrestricted eZ80 register or GPIO manipulation. The proof of concept and v1
do not require a bomb-proof privilege system; direct manipulation remains
unsupported caveat emptor.

The Author clarified that EMOS route, transport, activation, and mode ownership
is normative and inviolable guidance for every project firmware, software, API,
example, test, review, and user document. It is not a privilege boundary or a
non-bricking warranty for arbitrary external eZ80 code. A programmer who
bypasses EMOS through direct GPIO, peripheral, protocol, interrupt, flash, or
routing manipulation violates the supported contract and may corrupt, damage,
or brick either system. This narrows the earlier pre-activation
non-destruction wording to fail-safe requirements on project-produced designs
and supported states.

The Author accepted REMED-001 `W2D-Q09.9`: “EMOS is boss.” EMOS is the sole
authority that names the current formal mode and does so only from committed
state. EDP/P4 firmware and applications may report local, requested, pending,
readiness, transport, or failure state, but must not name an uncommitted,
failed, or partial combination as a formal mode.

The Author accepted REMED-001 `W2D-Q09.10`. The EMOS mode-transition
coordinator must complete a committed return to Legacy before attempting any
different non-Legacy destination. Direct non-Legacy transitions require later
separate architecture, implementation, and qualification approval. This closes
Q09, REMED-001 Work 2.d, and SETUP-005-D002; no implementation, procedure,
qualification evidence, wiring, or hardware changed.
