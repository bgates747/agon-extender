# Development log — 2026-08-21

## ADR lifecycle and EDU operating modes

Established separate ADR `Status` and `Completeness` metadata. Status records
whether written decisions are authoritative; completeness records whether known
architectural decisions remain unresolved within the ADR's declared scope.
Normalized ADR-0001 through ADR-0013 as accepted and complete while preserving
qualification state separately.

Renamed and expanded ADR-0014 to cover EDU operating modes, state ownership,
and service architecture. Accepted exclusive compatibility mode as the sole
mode eligible for complete untouched-legacy compatibility, and extended
cooperative mode with VDU primacy over canonical MOS sysvars and a separate EDU
result domain. ADR-0014 remains partial; SETUP-004 owns stable open-decision IDs
for transparent EDP routing, mode lifecycle, response delivery, and legacy
abstraction boundaries.

Clarified the deployment boundary: baseline extended cooperative mode must work
with stock MOS through EDU-aware linked code or the optional resident service.
An Extender-enabled MOS is reserved for system-integrated capabilities such as
a MOS-based transparent legacy-routing implementation and is not a general EDU
or cooperative-mode prerequisite.

Classified buffered command 128 as a supported local diagnostic rather than a
protocol return. The P4 port will route its `force_debug_log()` output to an
explicit EDP diagnostic sink, not MOS/sysvars or EDU results, and must account
for the upstream command's synchronous whole-buffer output and resulting stall
or watchdog risk.

Closed SETUP-003 after acceptance of all seven structural-inventory work items.
Updated SETUP-004 to show that its VDU compatibility inventory is complete while
the subsystem disposition matrix remains pending. Moved ADR-0014's five open
system-integration decisions and routing analysis into new task SETUP-005 so
they do not block completion of the source-disposition survey.
