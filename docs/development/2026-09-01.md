synchronous position/pixel reads are guarded, and raw plane views always
require a retained read/mutation lease except during permanently closed
exclusive teardown.

metadata need a separate short-held nonrecursive mutex with the sole lock order
frame-state then payload; retry/yield and queue send remain outside both. The
P4-selected retained-common seams cover submission-owner validation,

one-at-a-time payload allocation/release without asserting unreviewed

explicit pending amendments to D009 and ADR-0015, not silently selected
lifecycle changes.

queues raw bitmap, `CopyToBitmap`, tile-backing, and oversized managed-path

bitmap clear erases ownership before sprite detach, and all-buffer clear fails to
reset sprites before destroying backing. This definite upstream-origin defect
is now `INTEGRITY-AUDIT-F022`, pending disposition to PORT-003 through D011.

skips, and which future logical edge would otherwise execute the work.

executor preflight before `running=true`, same-owner balancing on every start,
configure, and rollback exit, a post-frame-join/pre-background-disable pending-
snapshot settlement phase, and distinct ordinary-mode-change versus final-
destruction lease rules. The official pixel-read FIFO flush is retained before
