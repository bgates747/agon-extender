## AUDIT-002 — Wiring diagram accepted

At 15:00 EDT the Author accepted and closed AUDIT-002. A deterministic Fritzing
connectivity extractor, evidence-derived expected-circuit manifest, and
comparison validator exposed missing Agon pin 33/GND and pin 34/+3.3 V entry,
an incorrect bidirectional PC1/U1 route, three dangling pull-up leads, U1 VCC
and bypass capacitance on ground, and one physically double-occupied CLOCK
hole. The generated `draft_v1` corrects those defects while preserving the
original Author draft and unrelated geometry.

`207a59b795b472db7881b68eadeb7a55464ab13a51690bfa1de8f1476e8564cb`,
passes all 60 expected-circuit checks. Its complete non-wire terminal net

acceptance establishes a correct drawing of the controlled-power predecessor
circuit, not physical bench qualification or approval of that topology as the
new beta or production design.
