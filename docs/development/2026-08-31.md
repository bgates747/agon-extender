## PORT-008 external-ZDI failure capture

The P4 was temporarily repurposed as a three-wire external ZDI observer so the
Agon could retain its stock onboard VDP while reproducing the physical
VDP-banner/no-MOS-banner failure. After correcting P4 USB-console and GPIO
porting defects, run `PORT-008-2026-08-31-21-55-19Z` passed all identity gates
and captured the original failure without replacing the VDP.

The eZ80 stopped at `0x0009EA` in `_wait_timer0`, called by `_wait_ESP32` during
MOS startup. Timer 0 had reached zero and `gp` remained zero, so this is a
repeated General Poll timeout rather than a frozen timer. UART0 was enabled as
8N1 with hardware flow control, CTS accepted, transmitter empty, and no receive
byte pending. The evidence does not yet distinguish missing request delivery,
stock-VDP response behavior, baud/configuration error, or receive-path failure.

A post-capture P4 reboot changed the halted ZDI context; the supplemental image
refused its write-gated UART-divisor read as designed. After a manual target
reset, run `PORT-008-2026-08-31-22-27-18Z` repeated the original execution
state and read UART0 divisor `0x000B` within the same halt epoch. That divisor
exactly matches 24-bit overflow of the inherited `16 * 1,152,000` baud-product
under AgonDev; the intended 32-bit expression produces divisor 1. `agon-emos`
QUAL-001 now owns the narrow source correction and its emulator and physical
requalification gates.

The narrow widening correction then passed the full AgonDev machine suite and
the Author's graphical emulator review. EMOS reached the normal prompt,
reported its expected identity and providers, completed its service calls, and
completed Legacy to Dual to Legacy transitions. The source remains
uncommitted; replacement identity, clean build, and physical installation are
